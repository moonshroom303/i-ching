# Third-party content / Inhalte Dritter

## German text – `app/data/de.js`

Richard Wilhelm: *I Ging. Das Buch der Wandlungen.* Aus dem Chinesischen verdeutscht und erläutert.
Eugen Diederichs, Jena 1924.

Richard Wilhelm died on 1 March 1930. His translation has been in the public domain since
1 January 2001 (70 years after the author's death) and is public domain in the United States
as a work published in 1924.

Digitised text taken from the project **Yijing Oracle** by Johannes Kaindl
(<https://github.com/johannes-kaindl/yijing>, file `data/hexagrams.json`), whose licensing
statement declares the Wilhelm translation in that file public domain. Only Wilhelm's original
wording was taken over; that project's edited variants and added material were not used.
Hexagram names, pinyin and the line patterns of hexagrams 39 and 40 were checked and corrected
(see `scripts/build-data.mjs`).

## English text – `app/data/en.js`

New English translation of Wilhelm's 1924 German edition from the project **i-ching** by
Jess Hewitt (<https://github.com/jesshewitt/i-ching>), dedicated to the public domain under
**CC0 1.0 Universal** (<https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/>).

## Fonts – `app/fonts/`

Alegreya and Alegreya Sans by Juan Pablo del Peral, Huerta Tipográfica.
SIL Open Font License 1.1, see `app/fonts/OFL-Alegreya.txt` and `app/fonts/OFL-AlegreyaSans.txt`.

## Independence / Unabhängigkeit

Wandlungen is an independent project. It contains no code, design or text from commercial or
other I Ching websites apart from the sources listed above.

Wandlungen ist ein unabhängiges Projekt. Es enthält keinen Code, kein Design und keine Texte
von I-Ging-Webseiten, außer den oben genannten Quellen.
